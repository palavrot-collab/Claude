# Pillar

The three benefit pillars: a sage circle with a dark-green glyph, and a two-line green label beneath.

- Provide: one icon from the Icons asset group and a label of two to five words.
- Always three, in this order right to left: stress relief (lotus), resilience (sprout), connection (people and heart).
- Use on posters, the opening of a lecture, landing pages, and as the three categories of a dashboard.
- Don't: recolor the circles or mix in other icon styles.
