# SlideFrame

The 16:9 lecture slide in the forest line: logo top right, one idea in the middle, a green rule and footer at the bottom.

- Provide: eyebrow (session or chapter), one title sentence, at most one supporting line, footer text and slide number.
- Content slides on `surface`; chapter slides use `.yr-slide-deep` with the white logo.
- One idea per slide, title at most two lines. Photos go full-bleed on their own slide with a `.yr-frost` caption card.
- This is the frame for Shlomi's lectures in the forest line; the Mindset deck structure is kept, only the look changes.
- Logo: `.yr-logo-light` (forest) and `.yr-logo-dark` (white) side by side on content slides; the theme shows the right one. Chapter slides always use the white logo.
