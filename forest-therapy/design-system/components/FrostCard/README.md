# FrostCard

The signature composition: forest photography full-bleed, a translucent mist card centered on it, everything legible on the card.

- Provide: a photo (real forest or session photography, soft light, never stock-looking people posing), and the card content.
- `.yr-photo` is the photographic frame; `.yr-frost` the card. Text on the card uses `ink` and `ink-body`; the logo sits above the card, on the photo.
- Use for event posters, social posts, story frames and lecture title slides.
- Don't: put text directly on the photo, or make the card opaque.
