The visual and verbal line of רפואת יער ישראל (Forest Therapy Israel): a non-profit bringing research-based forest therapy to organizations, managers and people in recovery. Everything made for the brand (posters, social posts, motion, dashboards and lecture slides) is built from this system. Write in Hebrew, right to left, unless a piece is explicitly in English.

## Content fundamentals

- **Voice: an invitation, not a pitch.** Warm, calm, plural and direct: "אנחנו מזמינים אתכם למפגש בוקר חווייתי בטבע". Speak to the reader as אתם, never as a customer.
- **Lead with the reader's problem, answer with the forest.** A short question, then a short answer: "העובדים שחוקים? בואו ליער." One line each, ending with a period.
- **Gender-inclusive Hebrew.** Name both forms ("מנהלות ומנהלי משאבי אנוש") or use the dot form ("לבכירים.ות"). Never default to masculine only.
- **Research is the backbone.** Say "גישה מבוססת מחקר" and name the source when you state a number (Antonelli et al., 2019; the Helsinki-approved PTSD study led by Prof. Itamar Grotto, Dr. Efrat Danino and Galia Ben Haim). No number without a source.
- **English only for terms of art** the audience uses in English: People & Wellbeing, Unlearning, Sit Spot, PTSD, HeartMath.
- **The promise closes every piece:** בריאות טובה יותר | אנשים מחוברים יותר | ארגונים חיים יותר. Never reword it.
- **No emoji, no exclamation marks, no hype words** (מהפכני, חובה, אסור לפספס). The forest does not shout.
- Bold the brand name inside running text: "בו תכירו את **רפואת היער**".

## Visual foundations

**Color.** Three families from the logo and flyers:
- Forest: `brand` (logo green) for the logo, rules, dates and the main chart series; `brand-strong` whenever green is small text; `surface-deep` for chapter slides and research bands, with `on-deep` text.
- Bark: `ink` (headline brown) and `ink-body` for text; `bark` for the one primary call to action, with `on-bark`.
- Mist and sage: `surface` as the default ground, `frost` for the translucent card over photos, `sage` for icon circles, `sage-soft` for tinted panels.
- `leaf` is a sunlit highlight for one detail per frame (a highlighted value, a sparkle in motion). Never text on light grounds.
- `positive` and `caution` are for data states only, always with a sign, word or icon.

**Type.** Two Hebrew families from Google Fonts, both rounded and open like the logo's lettering:
- `display` (Heebo, 700 to 900) for headlines, dates, numbers and labels. Styles: `display-xl`, `display-l`, `headline`, `title`, `date`, `stat`, `label`.
- `body` (Assistant, 300 to 700) for paragraphs and speaker lines. Styles: `lead`, `body`, `body-light`, `small`.
- Headlines in `ink`, balanced, at most two lines. The flyer's airy second lines use `headline` at weight 500 or `body-light`.

**Imagery.** Real forest light: canopy, mist, dappled sun, people sitting in a circle among trees. Desaturated greens, soft focus, morning haze. Photos carry no text; text goes on a `frost` card (see FrostCard). The potted sapling with colored pencils is a recurring motif on event flyers (growth plus learning); place it at the lower corner, overlapping the card edge.

**Layout.** Centered, symmetric posters: logo at the top over the photo, the frost card in the middle third, DateLine under the headline, three Pillars, the CTA pill, the Tagline at the bottom. Dashboards and slides align to the right edge (RTL). Spacing from `space-1` to `space-8`; card padding `space-6` (`space-5` on phones).

**Shape.** Soft and organic: `radius-lg` for cards and photo frames, `radius-md` for panels and tiles, `radius-pill` for buttons and the date chip. Borders only as `hairline` on data panels. Shadows: `shadow-card` on the frost card, `shadow-pill` on CTAs, nothing else.

**Focus.** Every interactive element shows a 2px solid `focus` ring with a 2px offset.

## Motion

Motion moves at the forest's pace: slow, breathing, never bouncing.
- Durations 600 to 1200ms for elements, 6 to 15 seconds for a full loop or reel scene. Easing `cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)` (ease-out); no spring or overshoot.
- Text rises 12px while fading in, staggered 120ms by line. Numbers count up over 1.2s with tabular figures.
- Ambient layers: mist drifting sideways, leaves falling slowly, light rays pulsing at a breathing rhythm (4s in, 6s out, as in coherent breathing).
- Every piece ends on the logo and the Tagline, held for at least 2 seconds.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: show the final frame without movement.

## Data and dashboards

- One StatTile per finding: label, value with sign, meaning, source. A ResearchBanner opens a research page.
- Chart series: `brand` first, `sage` second, `bark` for a comparison or baseline, `leaf` for the single highlighted value. Grid lines in `hairline`, plot areas on `surface-raised`.
- Before and after is the brand's core story: show it as paired bars or a slope, before in `sage`, after in `brand`.
- Participant data is shown only in aggregate, never with names.

## Lectures

Lecture decks use SlideFrame: 16:9, one idea per slide, logo at the top right, a green rule and footer at the bottom. Content slides on `surface`, chapter slides on `surface-deep` with the white logo, photo slides full-bleed with a frost caption card. The structure of Shlomi's Mindset lectures stays the same; only the visual line changes to this one.

## Iconography

The brand's icons are solid dark-green glyphs inside `sage` circles: lotus (stress relief), sprout (resilience), people with a heart (connection). They are in the Icons asset group as 216px crops taken from the event flyer; replace them with the original vector files when available. Don't mix in outline icon sets or emoji. Anything beyond these three is set as text.

## Logos

Use `logo-horizontal-forest.png` on light grounds and `logo-horizontal-white.png` on photos and `surface-deep`. Keep clear space equal to the height of the letter י around it. Never recolor, stretch or place it on a busy part of a photo.
